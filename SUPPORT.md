# Getting help with ReplAI

For setup and integration guidance, start with the [ReplAI documentation](https://replai.kg/docs/). The [website](https://replai.kg/en/) explains the product and its features.

For product questions, account issues, or integration help, contact [support@replai.kg](mailto:support@replai.kg). Describe what you are trying to do, the integration involved, and the result you see.

For a reproducible software issue, use the bug report form in the relevant repository if you have access. Include reproduction steps, the version or commit, expected and actual behavior, and sanitized logs or screenshots when useful.

Public issues are visible to everyone. Keep credentials, customer conversations, personal information, and account-specific details out of public issues and attachments. Share sensitive details through the support channel instead.
