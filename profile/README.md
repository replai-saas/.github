# Your catalog. Your knowledge. Every conversation.

We build AI assistants for online stores. ReplAI helps shoppers find products, get answers, and place orders through website chat, Telegram, and WhatsApp.

Connected to a store's catalog and knowledge base, ReplAI brings product information and store policies into the conversation. Store teams manage conversations and orders in one dashboard and can step in whenever a customer needs a person.

## Built around your store

- **Catalog search** — help customers find products and compare prices, variants, and availability using your catalog.
- **Store knowledge** — answer questions about delivery, payment, warranties, and returns using your policies, FAQs, and documents.
- **Order collection** — gather product choices and customer details within the conversation.
- **Human handoff** — let your team take over with the conversation history at hand.
- **Store integrations** — connect catalog data through ReplAI Feed, including our OpenCart extension.
